Because the SNES hardware auto-joypad read shifts the 1st pulse (B) 
into the highest bit (bit 15) of the 16-bit register ($4218–$4219), 
the hexadecimal bitmasks map from 0x8000 down to 0x0010 as follows:

Pulse       SNES Register Bit   Button          Hex Mask
1st         Bit 15              B               0x8000
2nd         Bit 14              Y               0x4000
3rd         Bit 13              Select          0x2000
4th         Bit 12              Start           0x1000
5th         Bit 11              Up              0x0800
6th         Bit 10              Down            0x0400
7th         Bit 9               Left            0x0200
8th         Bit 8               Right           0x0100
9th         Bit 7               A               0x0080
10th        Bit 6               X               0x0040
11th        Bit 5               L               0x0020
12th        Bit 4               R               0x0010
13th–16th   Bits 3–0            (Unused / ID)   0x000F
